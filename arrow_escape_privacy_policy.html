Privacy Policy

Arrow Escape · Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Arrow Escape ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Arrow Escape is an offline puzzle game — we designed it to work without accounts, sign-ins, or the collection of personal information by us.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The App has no user accounts, no registration, and no server operated by us.

Your game data — the level you have reached, per-level records, energy, statistics, awards, the daily-puzzle streak and your settings — is stored only on your device using local storage. It never leaves your device through the App, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows advertisements served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. To deliver and measure ads, Google may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system, language)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks, rewarded-ad completions)

This collection is performed by Google under its own policies, not by us. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising on your device at Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on older Android versions).

3. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where required by law, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before personalized ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the App, where that option is available in your region.

4. Rewarded Ads

The App offers optional rewarded ads that grant in-game energy, or an extra heart to continue a level, in exchange for watching a video advertisement. Watching rewarded ads is always your choice and is never required to play the game. Energy and hearts are virtual items with no real-world value and are stored only on your device.

5. Google Play Services

The App uses Google Play services for ad delivery and Play in-app updates (checking whether a newer version of the App is available). Google Play may process device information as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Internet Access

The App requests internet access solely for the advertising and update features described above. All gameplay works fully offline, including level generation, the daily puzzle and your progress.

7. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The App contains no chat, no social features, and no user-generated content. Advertising is delivered through Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the App, please contact us and we will help address it.

8. Data Security

Because your game data is stored only on your device and we operate no servers, the security of that data is governed by your device's own protections. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date.

9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. For data collected by Google (such as the advertising ID), please use the controls described in Sections 2 and 3, or contact Google directly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if the App gains new features. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App, contact us.
