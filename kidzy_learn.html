Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11-01-2025

Introduction

Welcome to Kidzy Learn. Your child's privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you and your child use our app.

Information We Collect

We prioritize your child's privacy and ensure that no personally identifiable information is collected directly from your child. Our app may collect non-personal data such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Usage statistics and app performance metrics

Use of Collected Information

The non-personal data collected is used to improve the app's performance, enhance user experience, and provide better educational content. We do not sell or share any collected information with third parties.

Third-Party Services

Kidzy Learn uses the following third-party services to enhance functionality:

  • Firebase Analytics: Helps us understand app usage and performance. Firebase Analytics collects anonymous data for analytical purposes.
  • Crashlytics: Used for monitoring app crashes and ensuring app stability, without collecting any personally identifiable information.

Child Safety and Data Protection

We adhere to the guidelines of child privacy laws such as COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act). We do not collect or store personal information from children under the age of 13.

Parental Controls

We encourage parents to monitor their child's app usage and engage with the educational content. Kidzy Learn is designed to be a safe and secure learning environment for children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page, and we encourage parents to review it regularly to stay informed about our privacy practices.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
