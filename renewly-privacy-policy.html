Renewly Privacy Policy

Never miss an important renewal again.

🔒Privacy First, Always

Renewly is designed to be 100% offline and privacy-first. Your data never leaves your device. There is no account, no cloud, and no tracking — ever.

We do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal information. All processing happens entirely on your device.

💾Data Storage

All your records, documents, medicines, warranties, vehicles and reminders are stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage (Hive). You are in full control of your data at all times.

  • No servers are involved.
  • No cloud backup is performed automatically.
  • Backups you create are saved as local files that only you control.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all stored data.

🛡️Permissions

Renewly requests only the minimum permissions needed, and all are optional:

  • Camera — used only to scan documents you choose to capture for OCR.
  • Media / Photos — used only to attach images you explicitly select.
  • Notifications — used only to remind you locally before renewals expire.
  • Biometric — used only to unlock the app if you enable App Lock.

Renewly does not request access to your location, contacts, SMS, microphone, or phone.

📊No Analytics or Ads

Renewly contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. The app is completely free with unlimited usage and no premium tiers.

👶Children's Privacy

Renewly does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

✉️Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact the developer through the app store listing for Renewly.
