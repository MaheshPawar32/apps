Introduction

Thank you for choosing Maharashtra Bus Timetable. This Privacy Policy outlines how we manage your data and privacy when you use our app. We value your trust and strive to ensure your privacy is protected.

Disclaimer

This app, Maharashtra Bus Timetable, is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government entity. It is an independent application created to assist users by providing accessible information on bus schedules. The app does not represent official government data or services.

Warning

Maharashtra Bus Timetable provides bus schedule information as a reference only. Timetable information may change, and we advise users to arrive 10-15 minutes early to ensure they do not miss any buses. We are not responsible for any inaccuracies in the timetable.

Sources of Information

The bus schedule information provided in the Maharashtra Bus Timetable app is based on publicly available data from the following sources:

  • ST Bus Timetable
  • MSRTC Official Website

We strive to keep the schedule information as accurate and up-to-date as possible, but users are advised to verify details as they may change. This app is provided as a reference only and is not an official government service.

Third-Party Services

Our application uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics: Used to monitor and analyze app performance and user interactions. Firebase Analytics collects anonymous data to improve user experience.
  • Crashlytics: Used for error reporting and app stability monitoring. This helps us fix crashes and improve the app’s performance without collecting any personally identifiable information.
  • AdMob: Used to serve ads. AdMob may collect certain information to provide personalized ads based on user interests.

Data Collection by Third-Party Services

While we do not collect any user data directly, the third-party services mentioned above may collect data in compliance with their privacy policies. Please refer to the privacy policies of Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and AdMob for more details on their data handling practices.
