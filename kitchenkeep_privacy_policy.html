Privacy Policy

KitchenKeep — Smart Shopping & Pantry Inventory

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KitchenKeep ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

KitchenKeep is an offline-first application. Your shopping lists, pantry inventory, recipes, meal plans, events, budgets and settings are stored locally on your device using on-device databases. We do not upload this content to our servers.

2. Camera & Photos

If you grant camera or photo permissions, the App uses them solely to capture or select images for receipt scanning (offline OCR) and product photos. Image processing happens on your device. Images are not transmitted to us.

3. Notifications

With your permission, the App schedules local reminders (expiry alerts, low-stock alerts, event reminders). These are generated on-device and are not sent through external servers.

4. Analytics & Crash Reporting

KitchenKeep does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not track your usage or collect telemetry about how you use the App.

5. Advertising

The App may display ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. Data You Control

  • Export: You can export your data as a JSON backup at any time from Settings.
  • Delete: You can clear demo data or all data from Settings → Data. Uninstalling the App also removes locally stored data.

7. Children's Privacy

KitchenKeep is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, you can reach us through our app's listing on the Google Play Store.
