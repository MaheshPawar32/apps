Privacy Policy

LoanWise – Smart Loan Manager

Last updated: May 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LoanWise ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using LoanWise, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information you provide during sign-up (e.g., name, email, phone number).
  • Loan data you enter into the app (principal amounts, interest rates, repayment schedules, borrower or lender names).
  • Device information such as device model, OS version, and crash diagnostics.
  • Usage analytics to help us understand how the app is used and improve it.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the core loan-management features of the app.
  • To sync your data securely across your devices (if cloud sync is enabled).
  • To send important notifications about loan reminders and due dates.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app stability and performance.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Cloud-synced data is hosted on Google Firebase, which complies with major security standards. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

LoanWise uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Crashlytics, Analytics) – Google LLC
  • Google Play Services – Google LLC

These services may collect information used to identify you, in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Permissions We Use

  • Internet – to sync your data and connect to our backend services.
  • Notifications – to send loan reminders and important alerts.
  • Storage – to back up or export your loan data when requested.

6. Your Rights

  • You may access, update, or delete your account data at any time from within the app.
  • You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.
  • You may opt out of analytics and non-essential data collection from app settings.

7. Children's Privacy

LoanWise is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us to have it removed.
