🥚 Sky Yolk — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 July 2026  ·  App package: com.dev.sky_yolk

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sky Yolk mobile game ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We designed Sky Yolk to work offline and to collect as little data as possible.

1. Information we collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. We have no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own.

The following data stays on your device only:

  • Game progress (scores, coins, achievements, unlocked cosmetics)
  • Settings (sound, haptics, accessibility and display preferences)

This data is stored in the App's private local storage, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sky Yolk shows advertisements served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app package)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This collection is governed by Google's policies, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in ads
  • AdMob data practices

Your choices: where required by law (e.g. GDPR regions), the App shows a consent dialog before serving personalized ads, and you may decline. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Google Play services

The App uses the Google Play in-app update feature to check whether a newer version is available. This check is performed by Google Play on your device; we receive no data from it. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google Play processes data.

4. Purchases

All in-game currency (coins and gems) is earned by playing. The App's cosmetic items have no real-money price and there are no gameplay advantages for sale.

5. Children

Sky Yolk is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad serving is configured through Google AdMob, which supports family-oriented ad settings; if you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service used by the App, please contact us and we will help address it.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data — we do not possess any. The only third party that processes data in connection with the App is Google (AdMob and Play services), as described above.

7. Security

Game data never leaves your device. Communication performed by Google's SDKs (ads, updates) uses encrypted connections managed by Google.

8. Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR, CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data, such requests regarding advertising data should be directed to Google via the links in Section 2. You can erase all local game data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Sky Yolk → Clear data or by uninstalling the App.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
