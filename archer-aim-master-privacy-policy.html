Privacy Policy

Archer Aim Master · Last updated: November 5, 2026

The short version: Archer Aim Master has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your progress is saved only on your device. The game shows advertisements from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to deliver them. That part is explained below, and you can control it.

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Archer Aim Master ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or transmit any personally identifiable information. The App has no user accounts, registration or sign-in, and we operate no server. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, location, microphone or camera.

Information collected by Google to serve advertising is described in Section 3. Google performs that collection under its own policies; we never receive it.

2. What the App Stores on Your Device

So you can pick up where you left off, the App saves your game data in your device's standard app storage. It stays on the device, we cannot read it, and it is never transmitted.

  • Levels unlocked and completed, and the stars earned on each level
  • Coins, experience points and player level
  • Achievements and the rewards you have claimed
  • Bows and arrows you own and which ones are equipped
  • Daily challenge records, streaks and best scores in each game mode
  • Statistics such as arrows fired, targets hit, accuracy and play time
  • Settings: sound effects, music, haptics, reduce motion and whether you have seen the tutorial

None of this identifies you. It is deleted when you reset progress in the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows advertisements served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. To deliver and measure ads, Google may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, rewarded-video completion)
  • Diagnostic information about ad delivery and performance

For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Ads appear only in these places:

  • Banners at the bottom of the Levels, Achievements and Statistics screens. There is never a banner on the title screen or during play.
  • An occasional full-screen ad when you choose to move on to the next level in Classic mode, never more often than every few levels. Never while aiming or shooting.
  • An optional rewarded video, described in Section 5.

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the law requires it, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before personalised ads are requested. In those regions you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Data → Privacy options inside the App.

5. Rewarded Ads

During a level you may choose to watch a video advertisement to turn on the Aim Guide (a preview of the arrow's flight path) for that level. Watching is always your choice and never required to play. The same Aim Guide can instead be unlocked with coins earned in the game, which works with no internet connection.

Coins, bows and arrows are virtual items with no real-world value. They are earned by playing, stored only on your device, and cannot be bought. The App contains no in-app purchases.

6. Internet Access

The App uses the internet only for the advertising described above. There are no accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, analytics, crash reporting or other telemetry operated by us.

All gameplay works fully offline: level generation, physics, progress, the daily challenge, all game modes and the collection. Without a connection the game plays the same, simply without ads.

7. Google Play

Separately from the App, Google Play collects information about installations, updates and any reviews you choose to leave, under Google's Privacy Policy. We receive only the anonymous, aggregated statistics that the Play Console provides to every developer, such as install counts by country.

9. Children

The App is a general-audience archery game and is not directed at children under 13. It has no chat, social features, user-generated content or purchases, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Advertising is delivered through Google AdMob in line with Google Play policies. Please check the content rating on the store listing to decide whether the App suits a particular child.

10. Data Security

Your game data is stored only on your device and we operate no servers, so there is no database of ours to breach. Local data is protected by your device's own security; we recommend keeping your operating system up to date and using a screen lock. Advertising traffic between your device and Google is encrypted in transit.

11. Your Rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to its sale. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, export or sell. We have never sold personal information and do not intend to. For data collected by Google, such as the advertising ID, use the controls in Sections 3 and 4 or contact Google directly.

You can remove everything the App has stored at any time:

  • In the App: Settings → Data → Reset progress erases levels, coins, statistics and unlocks.
  • On Android: Settings → Apps → Archer Aim Master → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the App.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the App gains new features. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions about this Privacy Policy or the App? Contact the developer using the contact details shown on the App's Google Play store listing.
