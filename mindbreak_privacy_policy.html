MindBreak Privacy Policy

Summary

MindBreak helps you understand how many Reels and Shorts you watch and how you use your phone. It has no account, no login and no server. Everything MindBreak records stays on your phone. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Accessibility Service (Reel counting)

If you turn on the MindBreak Reel Counter, MindBreak uses Android's Accessibility Service API only to count the short videos you watch in supported apps (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat, TikTok, Moj, Josh, ShareChat, Likee, Snack Video, Chingari) and to show your live count over those apps.

It reads which app is in the foreground and screen layout signals inside supported apps: view resource IDs, list positions, list sizes and on-screen bounds. It does NOT read or store text, messages, comments, captions, passwords, contacts or media, does not take screenshots or record the screen, and does not perform any actions for you. From other apps it only notes the app package name to know that you left a supported app. The result (a number of videos per app per day) is stored only on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Usage access (screen time and unlocks)

With your permission, MindBreak reads Android usage statistics to show screen time per app, phone unlocks, first and last phone use and phone sessions. These summaries are stored only on your phone.

Display over other apps

Used only to show the floating Reel counter while a supported app is open.

Notifications

Used only to warn you as you approach and reach your daily Reel limit.

Advertising

MindBreak is free and shows ads from Google AdMob on some screens. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). In regions that require it, MindBreak asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change that choice in Settings > Data & Privacy. MindBreak never shares your Reel counts or usage data with AdMob or anyone else.

Data storage, export and deletion

All MindBreak data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone and is excluded from cloud backup. You can export it as a CSV file or delete all of it at any time in Settings > Data & Privacy. Uninstalling MindBreak also deletes it.

Children

MindBreak is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on the Play Store listing. You can contact the developer using the email address shown on MindBreak's Google Play listing.
